For the LCDWiki ES3C40P ESP32-S3 N16R8 with a 320×480 ST7796S IPS display and FT6336U capacitive touch. Not for Hosyond E32R40T.

Test firmware: the complete ESP32-S3 image builds successfully, but display, touch, SD, battery and audio have not yet been verified on a physical LCDWiki board. Flash only if you are comfortable testing experimental firmware and recovering your board.

Native ESP32-S3 USB; you may need to enter download mode before the board appears. LCDWiki source and testing notes.

Before flashing: disconnect other serial monitors, select your CYD USB serial port when Chrome asks, and keep power connected. The partition layout and bootloader are included for initial installs. Keep a backup of settings; flash layout migrations can erase stored data.
